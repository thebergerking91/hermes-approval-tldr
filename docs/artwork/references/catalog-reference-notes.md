Shared visual grammar: distressed near-black, classical engraved collage juxtaposed with electronic blueprint lines, intense primary accents, large typographic hierarchy. Adapt the print texture, palette and technical detail; replace the figure and subject with original human-decision imagery. Omit official labels, logos, proprietary identity, and copied figures.
